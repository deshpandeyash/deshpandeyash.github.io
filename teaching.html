Practical work on ad-hoc networks, wireless communication, and MAC/routing optimization. The lab combines hands-on experimentation with analysis of network behavior.

Teaching philosophy

I aim to connect theory to practice: students should understand not only how a protocol is designed, but also how its behavior changes under real system constraints and measurement limitations.
