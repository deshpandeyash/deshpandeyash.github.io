Yash Deshpande

Engineer and researcher working on computer networking, clock synchronization, and datacenter systems.

I am a Wissenschaftlicher Mitarbeiter at the Chair of Communication Networks at the Technical University of Munich (TUM). My work focuses on making networked systems more accurate, predictable, and measurable.
