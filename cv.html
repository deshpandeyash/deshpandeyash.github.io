Summary

Professional focus

Networking and systems researcher at the Technical University of Munich specializing in precision network time synchronization and mathematical modeling of network latency.

My work combines IEEE 1588 PTP/NTP, GNSS-referenced synchronization, oscillator and clock-drift characterization, hardware timestamping, stochastic latency models, simulation, and experimental validation on high-speed programmable networks.

  • Simulation & measurement: SimPy, ns-3, OMNeT++, empirical measurement, experimental evaluation
